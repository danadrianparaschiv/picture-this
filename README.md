# Picture This: AI-Generated Data Visualizations 🏎️

Welcome to **Picture This**, a curated gallery of interactive Formula 1 data visualizations. This project explores the intersection of data storytelling and Agentic AI, showcasing what's possible when human curiosity meets the analytical power of AI agents like Claude and Gemini.

---

## 🌟 The Collection

This gallery is organized into two thematic sections:

### 📚 Educația în România
Exploring systemic challenges and data-driven insights into the Romanian educational landscape over the last 25 years.
*   **[Criza educației din România](educatia_romaneasca_in_ultimii_25_ani.html)**: An analysis of chronical underfunding, system-wide instability (ministers), and long-term exam results.
*   **[Criza resursei umane](romania_criza_profesori.html)**: A deep dive into teacher aging, qualification gaps, and salary challenges in the pre-university system.
*   **[Criza resurselor materiale](romania_criza_resurse_materiale.html)**: Infrastructure degradation, utility gaps, and material deprivation among students.

### 🏁 Hobby: Formula 1
A curated set of interactive visualizations exploring F1's history, engineering, and record-breaking drivers.
*   **[River of Points](F1-constructors-river-of-points.html)**: An immersive streamgraph of Constructor championship points (1990–2025).
*   **[F1 Youngest Legends](f1_youngest_records.html)**: Tracking the youngest prodigies to reset F1 milestones.
*   **[Grand Old Masters](F1_grand_old_masters_the_oldest_to_achieve_it_all.html)**: A tribute to the longevity and wisdom of F1's oldest drivers.
*   **[Pursuit of Speed](f1_lap_time_evolution.html)**: Analyzing over 50 years of lap time evolution at iconic circuits.

## 🤖 Built with AI

Every visualization in this collection was **conceptualized, designed, and coded by AI agents**. 

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This project is created for **fun and learning purposes**. While we strive for precision, these visualizations **may contain inaccuracies** in data points or historical context. They are intended as artistic explorations of data rather than definitive historical records.
