# Picture This: AI-Generated Data Visualizations 🏎️

Welcome to **Picture This**, a curated gallery of interactive Formula 1 data visualizations. This project explores the intersection of data storytelling and Agentic AI, showcasing what's possible when human curiosity meets the analytical power of AI agents like Claude and Gemini.

---

## 🌟 The Collection

This repository features a growing collection of interactive visualizations, each designed to highlight a different facet of Formula 1 history:

### 1. [River of Points](F1-constructors-river-of-points.html)
An immersive streamgraph visualizing the rise and fall of Constructor championship points from 1990 to 2025. Track team lineages through mergers and rebrands, from Stewart/Jaguar to Red Bull, and Benetton/Renault to Alpine.

### 2. [F1 Youngest Legends](f1_youngest_records.html)
A deep dive into the record-breaking achievements of the sport's youngest prodigies. From Max Verstappen's debut win at 18 to Kimi Antonelli's 2026 breakthroughs, see who reset the clock on greatness.

### 3. [Grand Old Masters](F1_grand_old_masters_the_oldest_to_achieve_it_all.html)
A tribute to longevity and wisdom in the cockpit. This visualization tracks the oldest drivers to achieve poles, podiums, and championships—back when F1 was as much about experience as it was about speed.

### 4. [Pursuit of Speed](f1_lap_time_evolution.html)
Analyzing over 50 years of pole position lap times at Monaco, Spa, and Monza. Witness the relentless evolution of engineering as teams find more speed despite decades of regulation resets.

## 🤖 Built with AI

Every visualization in this collection was **conceptualized, designed, and coded by AI agents**. 

> [!IMPORTANT]
