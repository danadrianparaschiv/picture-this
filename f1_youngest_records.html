Key takeaways

Vettel appears in all three lists — youngest pole, youngest win, and youngest champion. His 2008 Monza weekend for Toro Rosso (pole + win at 21) was one of the most stunning breakthrough performances in F1 history.

Antonelli just crashed into the top of the poles list and #2 on the wins list at the 2026 Chinese GP — at 19 he's nearly two full years younger than anyone else who'd taken pole before him.

Verstappen holds the race win record at just 18, on his debut race for Red Bull in Spain 2016 — a record that may never be broken given current super licence age rules.

The gap between categories is telling: the youngest pole came at ~19, the youngest win at ~18, but the youngest champion at ~23 — showing that sustained consistency over a full season takes a few more years of development even for prodigies.
